Network (LAN)

          

            Shows nearby devices from what Windows already knows (ARP/neighbors)—same idea as opening your router’s
            device list. It does not hack your network or read other devices’ traffic.
          

          

            Optional “probe LAN” sends brief pings across your subnet so quiet devices show up.
            Some routers or security apps label that as a scan—leave it off on shared or family Wi‑Fi unless you need it.
